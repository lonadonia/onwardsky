# Surface: Onward Sky homepage, header through "Be ready when proof of onward travel is requested"

Mode: Persuade. Audience: visa applicants, one-way travelers, travelers facing immigration checks, last-minute fliers (see PRODUCT.md). Job: start a route search in the hero booking form and hand it to the booking wizard. Proof on hand: verifiable PNR, auto-expiry, human support, 100+ airlines with nine named examples, Trustpilot 4.0 and three review excerpts, from $9.99, delivered in 2 minutes. Constraints: form visible above the fold on desktop, every form capability preserved, no invented claims, scope ends with the "Be ready" section. Decision process: client brief asked for three directions evaluated internally; roll ran degraded (service unreachable) and assigned candidate 5, which also scored highest on the brief's eight criteria.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a flight plan being drawn: a precise route chart where the booking console is the instrument and the traveller's own route appears in it as they type. It refuses the floating form card on a pale gradient with a dotted globe and a clip-art plane.

OWN-WORLD: Cool chart-paper ground with a hairline Lambert-conic graticule; a deep night-chart teal field that carries white type, its own graticule and cyan airways; burnt orange reserved for the action, the plotted route and the single proof mark in each illustration (carried from the incumbent illustrations); condensed chart lettering (Archivo ~74% width, heavy) for headlines, IATA identifiers and prices, Figtree for reading and controls; identifier boxes, waypoint markers and hairline rules as the only ornament.

STORY: In one line the visitor learns what they get; price, delivery and secure checkout sit beside the action; choosing airports turns the console into their itinerary (codes, route line); proof follows in order: verifiable PNR, auto-expiry, human support, 100+ airlines, real review excerpts; then they find their own checkpoint (visa, check-in, take-off, arrival) and act.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light header (logo, five links, WhatsApp support, orange CTA). On chart paper, a three-line condensed headline (~90px; lines break on meaning: "Get a verifiable / onward ticket / in 2 minutes.") spans the left seven columns; the lead, three proof points and the TrustScore line sit in the right five columns on the headline's baseline. Below, a full-container white console: trip type left and the fare (From $9.99, delivered in 2 minutes) right on top, so the price sits above the action; From ⇄ To, dates, passengers and the orange "Get my onward ticket" on one row; secure checkout, email delivery and payment marks beneath. A night-teal field rises behind the console's lower half and carries the airline identifiers.

FORM: Aeronautical enroute chart × Onward Sky brand; position 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:9e5708a9b0030f67. Raises: wayfinding signage (clarity: one message per panel, large legible labels); itinerary document (prove, don't claim: the console assembles the real route).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: choosing an airport turns its field into a chart identifier (code + city) and, once both ends are set, the route line between them draws in orange with the swap control on it.
Motion grammar: one authored load moment (the airway draws behind the headline), then motion only in answer to input; reduced motion shows final states.

Adaptations recorded after review: Mona Sans was replaced by Archivo after the design detector flagged it as an overused face; the hero airway and compass rose were removed because they crossed the copy at every width (airways now live in the night band and the journey's flight profile).
