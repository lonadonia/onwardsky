# Design System: Onward Sky

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Boarding Pass"**

The page is drawn as one family of tickets. The hero is drawn with vectors only, on a panel that grows with the screen to 1640px: on the brand-teal panel, a dot-matrix map of the world's land (Natural Earth's open data) carries five flight routes between real airports, four airport codes in small departure-board tags and one airplane in flight, beside the headline at the left; a boarding-pass-shaped booking bar is laid across the panel, the three promises and the Trustpilot score follow as one quiet glass bar, and the airline logos run along the panel's foot like a departures board. The reviews open with the Trustpilot score on a small ticket and continue in three review tickets built like the client's stubs, each with a picture drawn the client's way; the four "Be ready" cards are the client's own ticket stubs on the same dotted map. One idea, repeated with the same geometry, from the first viewport to the last section.

Every colour is a production colour. The palette is the production theme's presets and the values its section styles resolve to (teal ink, brand teal, deep teal, a soft teal surface, one burnt-orange action) and nothing is invented. Type is the production face, Figtree, at weight 800 for the hero and 700 for sections. The artwork (the four illustrations, the nine airline logos) is the client's own; the map is computed from public-domain data by a script, the icons are Phosphor's, and the three review pictures are composed by a script from Tabler Icons in the construction of the client's illustrations, so nothing is drawn by hand and there is no photograph.

Density is calm: large type, generous space, surfaces that change by meaning (brand teal for the welcome and the instrument, white for reading and operating, the soft teal surface for every ticket stub, a pale teal ground for "Be ready"). Motion is one entrance, one slow marquee that can be paused, and answers to input.

**Key Characteristics:**
- An inset, rounded brand-teal panel on white; its content shares one left edge with the header and every section.
- The booking form is a boarding pass (desktop): a white main half and a tinted stub with the fare and the CTA, a dashed perforation and two notches through which the panel shows. Below 1180px it becomes a white card.
- One picture, and it is information: routes between real airports, the client's own dotted-globe motif made precise. It is masked out across the text column, so text never sits on it.
- Ticket vocabulary everywhere: dashed perforation, semicircle notches, a barcode bar, small-caps stub labels, departure-board codes.
- Burnt orange appears only on actions and on error text (the production's own choice).
- A sideways airline-logo marquee as the foot of the panel; it pauses on hover and focus (no pause button, at the client's request).

## Colors

A teal-tinted palette with one hot action colour, taken from the production site.

### Primary
- **Action** (action): primary buttons only. Hover deepens to action-hover, press to action-active; action-active is also the error text colour, as in the production form.

### Secondary
- **Accent** (accent): links, icons, focus, route markers in the form, the dot texture behind "Be ready". **Deep** (deep): the panel gradient, the booking-card header and the selected tab.

### Tertiary
- **Accent Bright** (accent-bright): the routed connector in the form and the soft glow behind the map. **Aqua** (aqua): the key word in the headline, the map's routes and markers, icons on dark. **Mint** (mint): the illustrations' blocks and a reviewer's initials.
- **Peach** (peach), **Peach Wash** (peach-wash), **Orange Text** (orange-text): illustration warmth and a reviewer's initials, the error ground, the audience labels on the stubs.

### Neutral
- **Heading** (heading) for headlines, field values, the glass of the proof bar and the fill of the map tags; **Ink** (ink) for base text; **Secondary** (secondary) for body copy and quotes; **Muted** (muted) for labels and meta.
- **Surface**, **Surface Alt**, **Surface Soft**, **Section Ground**: every ticket stub (booking, Trustpilot, reviews, "Be ready"), tiles, a reviewer's initials and the "Be ready" ground.
- **Border**, **Border Strong**, **Divider**, **Section Line**: hairlines, perforations, rules.
- **Control Line** (control-line): every control boundary (3.3:1 on white). The only derived colour: muted mixed 70% with white.
- **Strip Start**, **Strip Ink**, **Deep Section**: the airline strip gradient (to accent) and its text; the end of the panel gradient; the scope strip.
- **Ticket Bar**: the barcode strip. **Placeholder**: placeholder text. **On Dark**, **On Dark Muted**: production values for text on dark cards, unused on this page since the dark review card was retired.
- **Trustpilot Green** (trustpilot-green): third-party rating squares, as the client's reviews already use.

### Named Rules
**The No New Colour Rule.** If a value is not in the production palette it does not ship. `tools/palette-audit.js` renders the page and lists every colour painted; the map is drawn in white, aqua and accent-bright only.

**The One Hot Colour Rule.** Burnt orange marks the action and the error; never a heading, badge or background wash. Errors are never red.

**The Boundary Rule.** A control's edge uses control-line (3:1 minimum); hairlines are decoration and never the only boundary of something a person operates.

## Typography

**Font:** Figtree variable (with the platform sans). One family, as in production.

**Character:** Round, friendly geometry at heavy weight for names, codes and prices, regular weight for sentences. The hero breaks on meaning and sets its key word in aqua, as the live site does.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(2.375rem…3.875rem), line-height 1.03, -0.035em): the hero headline only.
- **Headline** (700, clamp(1.875rem…3.125rem), line-height 1.06, -0.028em): section titles, balanced.
- **Title** (700, clamp(1.25rem…1.4375rem)): ticket questions; **Review Title** (700, 1.25rem). **Stage** (800, clamp(1.375rem…1.75rem), uppercase): the word on each ticket stub.
- **Rating Score** (800, 2.75rem, tabular lining numerals): the TrustScore in the reviews summary.
- **Price and Code** (800, 2.125rem and 1.625rem, 1.5rem on small phones, tabular lining numerals): the fare and chosen airport codes; the map tags use the same weight at 0.75rem with 0.08em tracking.
- **Lead** (400, clamp(1.0625rem…1.25rem), line-height 1.55): the hero promise and section intros. **Body** (400, 1rem) and **Body Large** (400, 1.0625rem): descriptions and quotes.
- **UI** (650, 1.0625rem), **UI compact** (600, 0.9375rem), **Button** (700, 1.0625rem) and **Label** (600, 0.8125rem, sentence case).
- **Caps** (800, 0.75rem, 0.14em) and **Strip Title** (700, ~0.8rem, 0.14em, uppercase): the stub labels and the airline strip title, which the production site already sets in capitals.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** Figtree only. Weight, size and colour create hierarchy, never a second typeface.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings, labels and buttons are sentence case; capitals are reserved for airport codes, stub labels and the strip title.

## Layout

A 1320px container (1240px until v6) with fluid gutters (16px on phones to 56px). The hero panel is inset by 8-20px and grows to 1640px; its content box is the page container, so it lines up with the header and the sections below at every width, and on wide screens the panel reaches past the content by up to 160px on each side (1408px wide at 1440, 1502px at 1536, 1640px from 1680). From 1180px: headline and lead at the left, the route map at the right (masked out across the text column), then the trip tabs and the boarding-pass bar across the full width, the assurances and payment marks, the glass proof bar, and the airline strip as the panel's foot. From 900 to 1179px the copy and a 430px card sit side by side, the card set 44-84px lower so the map shows above it (only the London tag, since the card covers the rest); below 900px everything stacks and the map is not shown. The header folds into the menu below 1200px. At 1440x900 the whole hero is above the fold.

The ticket's main half runs on one row for a one-way trip: route (From | To with a swap control between them), travel date and passengers. Return puts the route on its own row, then departure, return and passengers; multi-city stacks flights (2-6), one per row, each with a reserved remove column so the first flight lines up with the rest. The stub holds the fare above the submit button. The tablet and phone card runs on a two-column grid: route (From over To) full width, date and passengers side by side, fare, submit, status and foot.

Reviews open with the heading at the left and the Trustpilot ticket at the right (stacked below 900px), then three upright review tickets in a row from 1100px, one column of tickets lying down (stub at the left, as "Be ready") from 640 to 1099px, and upright tickets in one column on phones. "Be ready" carries its action at the right of the heading row (full width on phones), then a two-column grid of equal tickets on desktop and a single column below 1024px; on phones each ticket's stub becomes a compact row.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat and tonal; depth comes from colour fields and from the booking card lifting off the panel. Shadows always carry an offset and a soft blur tinted from the heading colour.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,47,52,.06), 0 14px 28px -14px rgba(14,47,52,.28), 0 40px 72px -36px rgba(14,47,52,.36)`): the tablet and phone booking card.
- **Ticket half** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,47,52,.2), 0 24px 38px -12px rgba(14,47,52,.45)`): the two halves of the desktop boarding-pass bar, lifted off the panel.
- **Pop** (`0 2px 6px rgba(14,47,52,.08), 0 22px 44px -18px rgba(14,47,52,.34)`): the suggestions list.
- **Ticket** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,47,52,.04), 0 22px 40px -34px rgba(14,47,52,.45)`): the review tickets, the Trustpilot ticket and the "Be ready" tickets (the production shadow).
- **Action** (`0 1px 2px rgba(122,51,0,.28), 0 8px 18px -10px rgba(201,67,0,.6)`): primary buttons (the production shadow).

## Shapes

Radii follow the production geometry: 8px chips, tabs and map tags, 10px buttons and icon tiles, 12px controls and the proof bar (11px for fields nested in a 1px-bordered group), 16px menus and the phone card, 18px for the tablet card, the review tickets, the Trustpilot ticket and every "Be ready" ticket, 22px for the outer corners of the desktop boarding-pass bar, 28px for the hero panel (22px on phones). Circles are reserved for the swap control, route and map markers, notches and reviewers' initials.

- **Shape:** 10px radius. **Primary:** action orange, white text, 700 weight, 48px (44px compact, 54-58px in the form and beside the "Be ready" heading); a trailing arrow nudges right on hover. Focus: 2px heading outline offset 3px; press drops 1px.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the booking form the brightest, most finished object in the first viewport, with its CTA above the fold.
- **Do** use only production colours, under their production names, and re-run the palette audit after any change.
- **Do** carry the ticket vocabulary (perforation, notch, stub label, departure-board code) into every new component.
- **Do** keep the airline marquee pausable and give reduced-motion users a static row.
- **Do** give every control a 44px minimum target and a control-line boundary, and every text link at least 24px.
- **Do** rebuild the map with its script (open data, computed routes) instead of editing or redrawing it.
- **Do** build any new illustration with `tools/build-review-art.py`: objects from Tabler Icons, unchanged, in the client's construction (blocks, 2.4 ink line, white paper, one orange accent, the wobble filter).

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a colour, a second typeface, a photograph or a stock illustration; the artwork is the client's, the map is computed from open data, the icons are Phosphor's and the review pictures are Tabler objects composed in the client's style.
- **Don't** use red for errors, gradient text, or orange for anything but actions and errors.
- **Don't** let the map reach the text: it is masked out across the text column and hidden below 900px, so the headline, lead and tabs keep 4.5:1 or better (3:1 for the large aqua word), measured on the rendered page.
- **Don't** fade text or the form while they animate; entrance effects slide them without changing their contrast (only the map behind them fades in).
- **Don't** animate on hover anything that is not a link or a button.
